1. Purpose

This document records the formal User Acceptance Testing sign-off for the Customer Onboarding / CIF & Account Opening module of the Digital Onboarding System. It confirms that the solution has been reviewed by the designated business stakeholders and is ready for operational handover, subject to the decision and approval of the KYC Unit.

2. Objective

The objective of this sign-off is to confirm that the onboarding solution:

  • supports the required maker and checker onboarding workflow
  • captures and validates key KYC information
  • supports CIF creation and account opening processing
  • provides visibility of customer information, images, signature, and supporting documents
  • supports operational review, approval, and reporting requirements
  • is ready for controlled business use in line with the agreed UAT scope

3. Scope of UAT

The UAT scope for this sign-off covers the onboarding module only and includes:

  • FAN / Fayda verification flow
  • OTP verification flow
  • customer profile retrieval and display
  • customer photo handling and verified profile generation
  • CIF data capture and validation
  • account opening data capture
  • funding source selection and validation
  • photo and signature upload flow
  • maker submission to checker
  • checker approval and account creation flow
  • approved account review and follow-up
  • onboarding reporting and tracking
  • KYC review workflow and visibility

Out of Scope for this sign-off

  • Telebirr Agent Transfer module
  • external production environment cutover activities
  • infrastructure, firewall, and network readiness outside the application itself

4. UAT Summary

Based on completed User Acceptance Testing activities, the onboarding module has been reviewed against the agreed business and operational workflow. The implemented solution supports the expected onboarding lifecycle from customer verification through account opening and post-approval monitoring.

The current version is considered:

  • functionally aligned with the agreed onboarding workflow
  • suitable for operational use in UAT
  • ready for business sign-off and handover decision by the KYC Unit

5. High-Level Functional Coverage Confirmed

The following key capabilities were verified during UAT:

  • maker can initiate and complete onboarding requests
  • checker can review and approve prepared onboarding requests
  • customer profile details can be captured and validated
  • verified profile can be downloaded for business use
  • customer image, NID image, signature, and supporting documents are visible during review
  • required onboarding controls can be enforced before submission
  • approved onboarding records can be tracked and reported
  • KYC review status can be tracked after account creation

6. Business Readiness Statement

The Digital Onboarding System onboarding module is hereby presented for sign-off to the KYC Unit as the receiving business owner. By signing this document, the KYC Unit confirms that:

  • the onboarding workflow has been reviewed in the UAT environment
  • the delivered scope is acceptable for business handover
  • the module is ready to proceed to the next release or deployment stage, subject to internal approval processes

7. Assumptions and Dependencies

This sign-off is based on the following assumptions:

  • required UAT test scenarios were executed by the responsible business users
  • external integration endpoints available in UAT were sufficient for business validation
  • any remaining deployment, infrastructure, or environment-level activities will be handled separately as part of release readiness
